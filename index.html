ShortsStudio

ShortsStudio is a private desktop application used only by the creator of Legends Deck. It renders our own videos on our own computer and uploads them to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok at scheduled times, using each platform's official API.

ShortsStudio is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. It requests only the permissions needed to upload and publish our videos (and, on YouTube, to add them to our playlists). Login tokens are stored only on the creator's computer.
